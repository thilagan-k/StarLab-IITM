Our Vision

"Hold on to the childhood curiosity that made us gaze at the stars and dream. Join us in quietly turning that wonder into meaningful research. Here at IITM STAR Lab, we envision developing sustainable and smart thermal energy systems. Let’s journey together from a spark of an idea to a tangible design and prototype, all while drawing inspiration from one another in creating a brighter future with renewable thermal solutions."
